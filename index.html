How it works

  1. Ask your assistant to write your profile. Copy the prompt below into Claude, ChatGPT or whatever you use. It writes a short profile from what it already knows about you. Read it, change anything you like.
  2. Paste it here with your first name and phone. That's your whole account. No app, no password, no photo.
  3. Get a text with a plan. When there's a good group for you, you get one invite with a time, a place and who's in. Yes or no. Then go.

The prompt

Paste this into the assistant that knows you best. The better it knows you, the better the invites.

I'm signing up for Burrito, a service that puts me in small groups of 3 to 5 neighbors for a real-life activity. Based on everything you know about me, write a short social profile for them: under 250 words, plain text, no headings, in the third person.

Cover: the neighborhood I live in; my rough life stage (for example new parent, recently moved, empty nester); five to eight interests I actually spend time on; the kinds of activities I'd realistically say yes to on a weekday evening or weekend morning, and the kinds I'd skip; when I'm usually free; three things I'd happily talk about for an hour; and how I like groups (quiet or lively, small or bigger).

Leave out my last name, employer, home address, health, finances, relationships, and anything I would not want a stranger to read. I'll review it before sharing.
